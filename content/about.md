The Duke University Debating Society is a group of students brought
together by an interest in argumentation, persuasion, and competitive
debate.

We've been around since 1897, making Duke Debate one of the oldest
student organizations on campus. Today, we practice throughout the
semester, compete at tournaments across the United States and around the
world, and spend a lot of time together outside of debate too.

::: split
## What We Debate

![Duke debaters during a round or
practice](assets/photos/about-debate.jpeg)

Duke competes in **British Parliamentary debate**, usually called BP.

A BP round has four teams of two speakers: two teams on the proposition
and two on the opposition. Teams receive the motion shortly before the
round and prepare their arguments without outside research.

Motions can cover politics, economics, philosophy, international
relations, culture, social movements, or something much more unusual.
You don't know what you'll be debating until shortly before the round.

## Who Joins?

Some of our members debated throughout high school in Public Forum,
Lincoln-Douglas, Policy, Extemp, Congress, Worlds Schools, or other
formats. Others came to Duke without any competitive debate experience,
and some of our most successful members started debating after arriving
here.

Many of the skills from other formats carry over to BP, but we don't
expect anyone to know the format before trying out.

## Thinking About Duke?

If you debate in high school and are wondering what opportunities you'll
have in college, or if you've already been admitted, here's what you
should know.

**Duke Debate has no role in or influence over Duke University's
admissions process.** We cannot advocate for applicants or otherwise
affect an admissions decision. Contacting us, meeting members of the
team, or being interested in Duke Debate will not have any effect on
your application.

If you've already been admitted to Duke, congratulations! Every student
interested in joining needs to go through our tryout process once they
arrive, regardless of previous debate experience. Our fall recruitment
is a chance to learn more about debate, meet current members, and see if
the team is something you'd like to be part of.
