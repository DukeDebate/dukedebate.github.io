Duke competes throughout the academic year, primarily on the North
American circuit, and sends teams and adjudicators to international
competitions.

Over the years, debate has taken Duke students across the United States
and to countries including India, Thailand, Greece, Turkey, Botswana,
Mexico, the Philippines, Singapore, Bulgaria, Vietnam, Panama, and 
Germany.
