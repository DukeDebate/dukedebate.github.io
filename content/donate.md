costs of competing and traveling to tournaments throughout the year.

If you're interested in supporting the team, thank you.

## Ways to Give

We prefer donations by **check or cash**, but we can also accept
**credit card payments**.

For information on how to make a donation, please reach out through our
contact page.

[[button: Contact Us -> #/contact]]

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Important Information

Before making a donation, please note:

-   Donations to Duke Debate are **not tax-deductible**.
-   Duke Debate does **not have a tax ID number**.
-   We **cannot accept wire transfers**.
-   We can provide a **W-9** upon request.
