Founded in 1897, Duke Debate is one of the oldest student organizations
on campus. We compete in British Parliamentary debate at tournaments
across the country and around the world, with members ranging from
experienced high school debaters to people who had never debated before
coming to Duke.

## Debate at Duke

![Duke debaters at a tournament](assets/photos/home-tournament.jpeg)

We practice every week during the semester and compete against
universities from across the United States and around the world.

You don't need to have debated in high school to join.

## Where We Compete

Duke regularly competes on the North American circuit and at the World
Universities Debating Championship. Debate has taken Duke students
across the United States and to four continents.

## The Team

![Duke Debate members traveling together](assets/photos/home-travel.jpeg)

We spend a lot of time together outside of debate, too.

Tournament weekends mean traveling together, exploring new cities, and
spending time together outside of rounds. Some of our favorite team

## Interested in Joining?

Tryouts take place once a year, during the fall semester, and are open
to Duke undergraduate and graduate students. Prior debate experience is
not required.
