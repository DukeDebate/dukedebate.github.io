Duke Debate recruits new members **once a year during the fall
semester**. Tryouts are open to all Duke undergraduate and graduate
students.

You do not need previous debate experience.

## Should I Try Out?

If you're interested in debate, we encourage you to try out.

Some applicants come in with years of high school debate experience.
Others have never debated competitively before. We don't expect
applicants to know British Parliamentary debate before trying out, and
we teach new members the format after they join.

::: split
## What Being on the Team Involves

### Practice

Members should plan to attend practice **at least once a week during
most weeks of the semester**.

Practices usually include debate rounds, drills, and feedback from other
members.

### Tournaments

We generally attend around **three tournaments per semester**, though
members don't attend every one.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## How Tryouts Work

### Information Sessions

We hold several information sessions during fall recruitment. These are
a chance to meet members, learn what BP debate looks like, and hear more
about the team and the tryout process.

### Asynchronous Tryout

The first stage of tryouts is asynchronous. Applicants submit a video of
themselves giving an argumentative speech.

### Interview

After reviewing the asynchronous tryouts, we invite selected applicants
to an in-person interview. The interview also includes an argumentative
speech.

### Decisions

We notify applicants after interviews are complete.

Specific dates and instructions change each year and are announced
during fall recruitment.
:::

## Frequently Asked Questions

::: accordion Do I have to travel?

No, if you do not want to. Individual tournament participation varies
throughout the semester. Members don't attend every tournament.

:::

::: accordion How are spots for selective tournaments decided?

Some tournaments have limited spots or are selective. For those, we 
may hold an internal tryout among members to determine who attends.

:::

::: accordion I'm an admitted Duke student. Can I participate before tryouts?

Congrats on your admission! Unfortunately, no. Once you arrive at Duke, our 
fall recruitment process is a chance to learn more about debate and meet 
current members. All students need to complete our regular tryout process 
before participating in club activities as members.

[Read more on our About page](#/about).

:::
