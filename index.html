This isn't the site

You've opened the project's placeholder file. The Duke Debate pages are built from the text files in content/ into a dist/ folder, and that folder is the actual website.

To see the site, run this in a terminal from the project folder -- it builds the pages and then serves them: 

./serve.sh

Then open http://localhost:8000/. Re-run it after you edit anything in content/. Full instructions, including how to add a page, are in README.md.

Nothing on this page needs JavaScript, and neither do the real pages -- their words are in the HTML. JavaScript only adds the extras: the dark/light toggle, the collapsing menu, and the photo gallery's filters and lightbox.
